Lucid design system

Every component below is painted by the shipping stylesheets in src/styles/.

01

The panel

One surface, every feature. The thinking state is the one that matters most: it holds the screen for the whole model round trip, so it gets the aperture, the spectrum ray, an aurora behind the title, and the shape of the answer sketched in before the answer arrives. Nothing blinks and nothing strobes - every loop is long, eased and alternating.

Quarterly revenue by region

The chart above breaks revenue down across four regions for the last four quarters. Figures are in millions of dollars and exclude intra-company transfers.

This column stands in for whatever page the user is actually on. The panel is a guest on it: it never takes focus captive, Escape always dismisses it, and it returns focus wherever it came from.

02

Reading Mode

The word marker advances with the voice. It is one element that travels rather than a box that vanishes and reappears, so the eye can follow it - within a line it glides, and at a line break it cuts, because easing a box diagonally across a paragraph looks like a bug. The solid bar sits below the baseline, so nothing is ever painted across the words being read.

Speaking

Lucid reads the page aloud and highlights each word as it is spoken, so someone who finds dense text hard to follow can listen and read at the same time.

The paragraph being read carries a soft wash with a rail on its leading edge. The word inside it carries amber. Two marks, two meanings, and they never trade places.

Word marker and paragraph wash are styled by the shipping src/styles/overlay.css. The gliding is src/design/reading-cursor.ts.

03

The chart badge

Pinned to charts and images Lucid can describe. A dashboard can carry a dozen of these, so at rest it holds perfectly still - it earns attention once when it arrives, then answers only to the pointer or the keyboard. Hover it, or tab to it.

04

Settings

The switch and the speech-rate slider are the two controls a demo actually puts a cursor on, so they are the two that get the attention. Saving a key is the one moment on this page that deserves a flourish.

05

Tokens

Ink and paper carry the interface. One accent for anything interactive. The aurora is spent only on "the model is working", so its appearance always means something. Amber belongs to reading and to nothing else.

Motion scale

Four durations, four curves, reused everywhere. Restraint is the point: one thing moves at a time.

To check the reduced-motion fallbacks: DevTools → Rendering → Emulate CSS prefers-reduced-motion: reduce. Every animation here turns off; none of the information does.
